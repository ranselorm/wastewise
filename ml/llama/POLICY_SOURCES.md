- Plastic recycling: Greater Cambridge Shared Waste
  https://greatercambridgewaste.org/recycling/recycling-plastic
- Item-by-item recycling directory: Greater Cambridge Shared Waste
  https://www.greatercambridgewaste.org/residents/what-goes-in-which-bin
- Household Recycling Centres: Cambridgeshire County Council
  https://www.cambridgeshire.gov.uk/residents/waste-and-recycling/household-recycling-centres/what-can-i-take-to-a-household-recycling-centre

Rules in `cambridge_policy.py` must be reviewed against these official sources
before they are changed. Dynamic services, such as food-waste collections and
small-electrical collections, require a current source check before release.
